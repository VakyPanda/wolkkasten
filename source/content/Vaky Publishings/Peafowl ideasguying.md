### **General and Spawning**
Peafowls spawn in groups. They have two variants - 'bluefowl' and 'brownfowl'. Bluefowl control brownfowl using chromatic feathers. 

Brownfowl can only breed in the presence of a bluefowl (and they will also lay eggs like chickens only in the presence of a bluefowl). 

Bluefowl can maintain a following group of up to 12 brownfowl. This is reflected in the number of feathers they have - less feathers = less brownfowl. More on feathers below. 

Bluefowl will generally NOT spawn with 12 feathers. The rarity of them spawning with more eyelets is 10% per feather slot. This means naturally, there will usually be 2-5 peafowl where one has eyelets to control the others. 

### **Behavior (Control and Combat)**
Bluefowl gain an eyelet (or chromatic) feather per brownfowl they control. Controlling more brownfowl has one benefit and one drawback:
- Feathers serve as armor, so landed attacks deal less damage and the feather fan acts as a giant shield that bluefowl use to parry attacks
- Feathers create severe drag, speed related to attack, movement, jump and flight is reduced. 

Bluefowl will fight each other upon interaction, and the losing bluefowl will be demoted to a brownfowl. A bluefowl loses by losing 75% of its total health. 
Bluefowl have two moves - parry and kick
- Parry uses the fan to deflect attacks, and the deflected % depends on how many feathers the bluefowl has accrued
- Kick is just a basic jump-kick which is influenced by the speed of the bluefowl 

Bluefowl fights are highly elaborate and are intended to be unpredictable, where the faster bluefowl may land quicker hits and do more damage, but the bulkier bluefowl may be able to parry these hits more effectively.  

Brownfowl without a controlling bluefowl will automatically fall under the control of the nearest bluefowl (within reasonable radius). An uncontrolled brownfowl is (randomly) capable of promoting to a bluefowl during rain.

### **Ranching**
Brownfowl have higher egg-laying efficiency than chickens. This efficiency also increases with a more feathered bluefowl. This makes peafowl ranching highly lucrative, but only with good management. Examples of bad vs good ranching are given below
- Exactly 12 Brownfowl, 1 Bluefowl - GOOD/NEUTRAL - Minmax, basically ensures maximum egg laying productivity with little conflict and no wasted fowl. 
- Multiple bluefowl - BAD OR GOOD - depends on the number of brownfowl. The group will automatically correct so that only one bluefowl remains. However, if there are more than 12 brownfowl, once the losing bluefowl is demoted, there will be one or more brownfowl that are not laying eggs. This arrangement also lets you farm bluefowl feathers though, which are much more difficult to stumble upon naturally.
- Too many brownfowl, not enough bluefowl - BAD - There will be unproductive brownfowl
- <12 Brownfowl - BAD/NEUTRAL - Egg laying efficiency of the whole group is decreased. However, allows the player to establish control over number of eggs produced.

### **Funny Item**
Bluefowl feathers are occasionally dropped during demotion. The **Chromatic Fan** is a new shield type item crafted using these feathers. The fan may be equipped with chromatic feathers (like a bundle) and used as a shield. When block is activated, the feather dress hoists up, creating a shield that blocks attacks. More feathers = more blocking ability, with 12 eyelets providing full knockback and damage resistance. Feathers do not have durability, and neither does the shield. 

Perfectly parrying enemy attacks has a chance to charm enemies, upon which one of the feathers is transferred to the player's crown. Tighter parries = stronger chance of charm. Charmed enemies gain an eyelet above their head and one of the shield eyelets extends outwards (or perhaps creates a crown or a expanded dress behind the player? idk?). These mobs are loyal to the player and follow the player around. However, for each gained eyelet, the player loses speed. Eyelets can be recanted by unequipping the shield, when the player dies, or when the mob dies. Controlled mobs will not fight back.
